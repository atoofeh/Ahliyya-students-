Privacy Policy

English العربية

This privacy policy applies to Feker/ فكر (com.alahliyya.lms), published on Google Play under the developer name VortexMind. It also covers the learning website at students-alahliyaa.com.

1. About the platform

Feker/ فكر is an online learning platform for students and instructors. The Android app is developed by Mustafa ALRefaee. “We” refers to the team that operates and supports the platform.

For privacy questions or requests about your personal data, email atoofeh8@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: name, username, email address, account role and status, profile image, and password credentials. Passwords are stored in a protected, hashed form.
  • Learning records: course enrollment, lesson activity and progress, quiz answers and results, assignment submissions and attachments, grades and feedback, course questions and replies, bookmarks, and certificates.
  • Files and course material: documents, images, videos, and other material uploaded by students or instructors.
  • Device and security information: IP address, browser and operating system details, app version, a pseudonymous device identifier, and a device fingerprint derived from browser and device characteristics. We also keep sign-in, session, and security or audit records. An IP address may indicate an approximate region; the app does not request precise GPS location.
  • Quiz integrity signals: during protected quizzes, the platform may record events such as leaving the quiz screen, changing tabs or windows, copy/paste attempts, leaving full screen, inactivity, refreshes, and network interruptions. Supported devices may report a screen-capture event; the platform does not receive a captured image through this feature.
  • Preferences and notifications: language and theme preferences, notification settings, and a device push token if Android notification permission is granted and push notifications are configured.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide courses, lessons, assessments, grades, certificates, and support; keep learning progress; send service notifications; administer the platform; investigate misuse and protect quiz integrity; and meet applicable requirements. Instructors and authorized platform staff may use learning activity to teach, grade, and support students.

4. When information is shared

  • Instructors and platform staff: authorized people can access learning records needed for teaching, grading, administration, and support.
  • Service providers: providers may process information for platform hosting, database and file storage, email delivery, and technical operations. Google Firebase Cloud Messaging may process a push token and notification data when push notifications are enabled.
  • Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed when required by law or needed to protect users or the security of the service.

We do not sell personal information, serve advertising in the app, or use the Android advertising ID for ad tracking.

5. Cookies and device storage

On the website, an HttpOnly cookie keeps your session signed in; local browser storage keeps preferences, a cached profile, and a pseudonymous device identifier. Quiz answers may be kept in temporary session storage so you can recover them while taking a quiz. On Android, refresh credentials are held in app memory or encrypted device storage, depending on the “Remember me” choice. Theme and language preferences are stored on your device. These technologies support the service and are not used for advertising.

6. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the platform, maintain learning records, provide support, protect account security, and meet applicable requirements. Some learning, audit, or security records may be retained after an account is disabled or deleted.

7. Security

We use access controls and technical safeguards designed to protect information. Connections to the service use HTTPS, and video media is encrypted at rest. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You can update profile and preference details that are available in the platform. Some learning, audit, or security records may need to be retained for operational or legal reasons.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the platform or its practices change. The latest version and its effective date will be posted on this page.
